PERMAPUBLISHING

Permapublishing applies the principles of permacomputing to publishing. It considers not only the final publication, but also the tools, formats, workflows, and infrastructures involved in producing and distributing it.

Antoine Fauchié proposes three principles: decoupling, deprecation, and empowerment.

These support content that can move between formats, longer use of existing tools and hardware, and publishing systems that people can understand, maintain, and adapt.

IN THIS GARDEN

  • content separated from its presentation
  • materials that can move between web and print
  • simple and reusable file formats
  • lightweight, maintainable tools
  • extended use of existing hardware
  • documented and adaptable workflows
Antoine Fauchié
