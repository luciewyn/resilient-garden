Field Note 01 — Previous Images Remain

Date: 2026-07-30 · Related bed: Bed 02 — Kindle Ghosting

[provisional — an early observation, not a tested finding]

On e-ink displays with disabled or partial refresh, remnants of a previous image can remain faintly visible beneath the current one. This note records that observation as a starting point for Bed 02; it does not yet describe a tested method or a confirmed result.
