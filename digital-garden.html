DIGITAL GARDEN

A garden is never completely finished. Things are planted, moved, cut back, forgotten, and sometimes found again.

This website grows in a similar way. It gathers notes, references, tools, experiments, and unfinished questions from across the project. Some pages are carefully tended. Others may remain sparse for a while. You can enter from different points and follow the paths that interest you. There is no single order for reading the garden.

As the project changes, pages may be revised, connected, or allowed to decay. Keeping the garden alive is part of the publishing process.
