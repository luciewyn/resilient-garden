ENERGY

This Garden runs on solar power.

A solar panel charges a battery, which supplies the energy needed to keep the hosting device online. Its availability depends on sunlight, stored charge, weather conditions, and the efficiency of the system.

Every page, image, request, and background process uses energy. This condition shapes how the website is built, hosted, accessed, and maintained. The limited power supply becomes part of the publication itself.

DESIGN RESPONSES

  • lightweight pages
  • reduced and dithered images
  • minimal network requests
  • locally stored assets
  • limited JavaScript
  • reused hardware
  • compatibility with older devices
