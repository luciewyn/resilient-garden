WAYS OF THINKING

PERMACOMPUTING Computing within limits.

Permacomputing asks how computers might be built, used, repaired, and maintained with greater attention to energy, materials, longevity, and existing resources. Instead of assuming that computing should always become faster, larger, and more powerful, it starts from what is already available and asks how much is enough.

→ Soil: Permacomputing
