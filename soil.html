About

SOIL

Soil gathers the ideas, references, and practices that support This Garden. It provides the conceptual ground for understanding digital publishing as a material system shaped by energy, hardware, software, labour, and maintenance.

Five areas made up this ground:

  • PERMACOMPUTING
  • PERMAPUBLISHING
  • COMMUNITIES OF PRACTICE
  • SCREEN MATERIALITY
  • DIGITAL GARDEN

Each offers a different way to examine how publishing can be designed, sustained, and adapted under limited conditions. Together, they guide the tools, experiments, and infrastructures developed throughout This Garden.
