The Greenhouse

[under construction — not yet built]

The Greenhouse is the planned interface for the garden's image-processing experiments: downsampling, bit-depth reduction, dithering, and related techniques applied directly to an uploaded image. It is shared infrastructure the garden beds will eventually draw on, rather than a bed itself.

This first prototype does not implement the interactive tool. The beds document their research directly for now; the Greenhouse will follow in a later version.
