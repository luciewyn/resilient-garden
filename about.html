≈≈ ABOUT THE GARDEN ≈≈

[ PROJECT ]

The Resilient Garden was initiated by Lucie (Wan-Yun) Lin as the web-based research environment of her master’s thesis, The Resilient Edition.

It brings together experiments in low-resource publishing, image reduction, screen materiality, and small-scale infrastructure. The garden grows through tools, material experiments, field notes, and a web-to-print edition.

[ CULTIVATED BY ]

Research, writing, design, code, and ongoing maintenance by Lucie (Wan-Yun) Lin.

Developed as part of the MA Digital Communication Environments at the Academy of Art and Design Basel FHNW.
