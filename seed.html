SEED

How might publishing grow under limited conditions?

A web page appears weightless. Behind it sit servers, cables, processors, batteries, screens, and the electricity that keeps them running — along with the continuous work of maintaining all of it.

These systems are usually presented as seamless and always available. The resources sustaining them stay out of view. This Garden begins there. It asks what happens when limited energy, ageing devices, reduced images, and small-scale infrastructure are treated as conditions to publish with, rather than problems to solve.

RESEARCH QUESTION

How can computational constraints, material traces, and low-resource infrastructures become editorial and visual strategies for resilient digital publishing?

WORKING PRINCIPLES

  • work within available resources
  • let technical conditions stay visible
  • reduce without losing meaning
  • reuse and maintain existing devices
  • keep tools understandable and adaptable
  • design for change, interruption, and decay
  • treat publishing as an ongoing practice
